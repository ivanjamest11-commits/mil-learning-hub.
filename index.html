What is Media and Information Literacy?

Understanding information in the digital world

Media and Information Literacy, or MIL, is the ability to access, analyze, evaluate, create, and communicate information using different forms of media.


MIL is important because people receive information every day through social media, television, websites, newspapers, videos, and other platforms.

Major Areas of MIL

Media Literacy

Understanding and analyzing messages created by different types of media.

Information Literacy

Finding, evaluating, and using information correctly and responsibly.

Digital Literacy

Using digital devices, applications, and online platforms effectively and responsibly.

Critical Thinking

Questioning information and checking evidence before believing or sharing it.

MIL Lessons

Lesson 1: Types of Media

Media can include print media, broadcast media, digital media, and social media. Each type can communicate information to different audiences.

Lesson 2: Evaluating Information

Before trusting information, check the author, source, date, evidence, and other reliable sources. Do not depend only on a headline or social media post.

Lesson 3: Fake News

False or misleading information can spread quickly online. Users should verify information before believing or sharing it.

Lesson 4: Responsible Media Use

Responsible media users respect other people, protect their privacy, avoid spreading false information, and think carefully before posting.

How to Spot Unreliable Information

  • Check the source.
  • Check who wrote the information.
  • Check the publication date.
  • Look for evidence.
  • Compare different reliable sources.
  • Be careful with sensational headlines.
  • Do not immediately share unverified information.
